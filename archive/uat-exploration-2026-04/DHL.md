# DHL Group Subsidiary Structure (as of 31 December 2025)

**Parent Company**  
**Deutsche Post AG (DHL Group)**  
*Germany, Bonn* – Listed parent company

**Important Note on 2026 Modernization (Hive-Down)**:  
In H2 2026 (after AGM vote on 5 May 2026), Deutsche Post AG will be renamed **DHL AG**. The Post & Parcel Germany division will be hived down into a separate wholly-owned subsidiary (**Deutsche Post AG neu**). This aligns the legal structure with the operational divisions for better governance and focus.

**Key Facts**:
- Almost all core operating subsidiaries are **100% owned** (direct or indirect).
- Total legal entities: Several hundred (full detailed list with equity and net income in the official PDF).
- Aviation network: Operated through dedicated subsidiaries (e.g., European Air Transport Leipzig GmbH and others).
- Recent focus: Healthcare logistics strengthened via CRYOPDP and SDS acquisitions.
