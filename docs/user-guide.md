# Sales Lead Research — User Guide

A friendly walkthrough for everyday use. No technical background needed.

## What this tool does for you

When you get a new lead, it shows you that company's whole **corporate family** —
its parent company and the smaller companies it owns — and tells you which of
them you **already sell to**, with their account number.

So instead of a cold call, you can walk in warm: *"we already work with your
parent company — here's the history."*

## Opening it

Open the web page your team set up. It looks like a chat window: a list of your
past chats on the left, and a box to type in at the bottom.

## Looking up a company

1. Type a company name in the box at the bottom — for example **FedEx** — and
   press Enter.
2. The tool finds the company and shows its family tree.
3. If several companies share that name, it shows a short list — click the one
   you mean.

## Reading the results

You'll see an indented list of companies (parent on top, the companies it owns
underneath). Next to each one is a coloured tag:

- **Green "customer" + an account number** — you already sell to this one. Pull
  up the account and use the relationship.
- **Amber "possibly … — verify"** — it looks like a customer but the name isn't
  an exact match. Worth a quick double-check before you rely on it.
- **Grey "not a customer"** — a fresh lead.

At the top it tells you **how many are already customers**, and **which report
the list came from and when** — so you can judge how up to date it is.

## If a company isn't found

Some companies (often non-US ones) don't file reports with the US regulator. If
the tool can't find one, it offers to **search the web** instead — click that
and it pulls what it can from the company's published reports.

## Your past lookups

Every company you look up is saved as a chat on the left. Click any of them to
revisit it. **"New chat"** starts a fresh one. (These are saved in your browser
on this computer, just for you.)

## Telling the tool who your customers are

For the tool to flag existing customers, it needs your customer list.

1. In the left panel, click **"Upload customer list"** and pick a spreadsheet of
   your customers.
2. The spreadsheet needs at least two columns: an **account number** and a
   **company name**. Other details (country, location, etc.) are optional.
3. Not sure of the format? Click **"download a sample"** to get an example
   spreadsheet you can copy.
4. Each upload **replaces** the previous list.

> **A note on privacy.** The list stays on the computer running the tool. If
> it's real customer data — which can include tax numbers — treat it as
> sensitive. For company-wide use, your IT team may prefer to connect the tool
> straight to your customer system rather than uploading a file.

## Good to know

- The family tree comes from the company's **latest public annual report**, so
  it's as current as that filing (the date is shown).
- The **"possibly — verify"** tag is a safety net for near-matches — always
  glance at those before acting on them.
- If no customer list has been uploaded yet, you'll still see the family trees —
  just without the account numbers.
